# Model 3 option collection and school planning policy — v0.46.0

The new entry point is Year 10 options (`#/options`). The source is the existing transcription of TT Model 3 from Timetable options 2025_26.docx. Blocks A–I have four weekly periods each. A–E each provide one 24-seat class per offered subject. Language and compulsory blocks have up to ceil(cohort/24) sections and are split into classes of at most 24 during blocking. This prevents compulsory English/Mathematics from becoming impossible for the 54-pupil cohort. A different allocation of option-class sections can be introduced later through school planning; these demo capacities are explicit in the form.

The student uses their own number login and sees only their own identity/record; staff can select a pupil and submit on their behalf. Names, group names and membership come from managed Directory records. Students cannot impersonate another pupil through the endpoint. Submitted-choice counts include active Year 10 pupils and are rechecked transactionally at submission. Revisions prevent lost updates. A former/inactive pupil no longer occupies a place. One subject per block, no subject twice, at least one Physics/Chemistry/Biology, and the source document's ** eligibility restrictions are validated before saving.

Admins can preview submitted choices as parallel teaching groups and open them as a separate planning draft. Partial cohorts are explicitly marked incomplete; other pupils are not assigned fabricated choices. A Year 11 continuation preview preserves IDs and choices while changing the planning year and year level. Future-year staffing is a scenario requiring review. The active MIS timetable is not changed by collection/importing a draft. The current planning draft can be recovered through existing Undo; active MIS records are untouched.

The policy checks maximum 24 per class; teacher total allocation <=30, HOD/HOY <=24; subject/year-band eligibility; maximum two pupil periods of the same subject per day; Monday Period 1 Assembly; and one PSHE period weekly for Years 7–9. Year 10 draft staff totals include their baseline other-year teaching commitments, so a Year 10 allocation is not mistaken for an entire weekly load. The department allocation tool balances total load and year-band exposure. It is a bounded heuristic, not proof of globally optimal fairness. The manager declares subjects, eligible year bands, and HOD/HOY responsibility in People & rooms.

PE remains a consecutive two-period lesson. The confirmed five-day rule is Year 7 Monday, Year 8 Tuesday, Year 9 Wednesday, Year 10 Thursday, Year 11 Friday. Primary may share days. In the new policy, consecutive teaching periods can span a break or lunch; paired singles are preferred. Model 3 cohort generation places each four-period option block as two doubles on different days, with shared core sessions occupying the remaining four periods.

Every Russian / Russian-as-a-second-language / Uzbek Language activity gets a synchronised non-specialist supervision reservation when the policy is applied. Managers use Language support pathways to move explicitly selected pupils to the parallel group. No language needs are inferred from names or nationality. Empty support reservations reserve staff/rooms without occupying pupil timetables. This is separate from the document's eligibility declaration. Once actual support pupils are selected, their memberships are tracked by existing student IDs.

The hosted demo uses the existing browser-local database namespace. Counts and submissions are shared between accounts in that browser only, not between pupils' separate devices. A shared backend is required before collecting a real cohort remotely. No billing changes were made.
